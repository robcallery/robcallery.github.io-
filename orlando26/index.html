Tipping and tax
Restaurants with table service
Tip 18–20%, as service is not included. Disney adds 18% automatically for parties of 6 or more.
Counter service and mobile order
Tipping is not expected.
Bars, Uber and Lyft
About $1–2 a drink or 18–20% on a tab. Rides are 15–20% and optional, added in the app.
Sales tax
Prices are shown before tax, which is added at the till. It is roughly 6.5% in Orange County (Disney, International Drive) and 7.5% in Osceola County (Kissimmee). Check your receipts.
Paying by card
Contactless works almost everywhere. If a card machine offers to charge you in pounds, choose dollars, as the pound option usually has a worse rate. Check your bank’s foreign fees.
Typical journeys from the hotel in Lake Buena Vista. Times are rough and change with traffic, so check your Uber or Lyft app.
Airport to hotel
Orlando airport (MCO) is about 30 minutes by car. Book an Uber or Lyft once you have your bags, ready for the 19:00 plan.
Magic Kingdom
Uber to the Transportation and Ticket Center (TTC), then the monorail or ferry to the gate. Allow an extra 30 minutes before opening.
EPCOT and Hollywood Studios
About 10–20 minutes by car, with a drop-off at the main entrance. The Skyliner gondolas connect EPCOT and Hollywood Studios with the Riviera, Caribbean Beach, Pop Century and Art of Animation resorts, so they suit resort hopping.
Animal Kingdom
About 15–20 minutes by car, with a drop-off near the main entrance. Go early, as the park opens at 8am.
Disney Springs
A short 5–10 minute drive. Parking is usually free there.
Orlando Vineland Premium Outlets
Very close to the hotel, about 5 minutes by car. Open 10am–9pm Monday to Saturday and 11am–7pm on Sundays.
Orlando International Premium Outlets (I-Drive)
About 20–25 minutes by car, up International Drive. Parking is free.
The Florida Mall
About 20–30 minutes by car, off the Orange Blossom Trail. Check the hours on the Opening hours tab.
The Mall at Millenia
About 20–30 minutes by car, near Universal. Check the hours on the Opening hours tab.
Food and shops near any of these
In Out & About, then Nearby, tap a chip to sort restaurants and stores from the hotel, either mall, either outlets or any of the four Disney parks.
Getting home from a park
Rides cost more and take longer to arrive right after the fireworks. Wait 20 to 30 minutes, or walk away from the entrance first, then book. Each park has a signed rideshare pickup area.
Disney buses
These mainly serve Disney resorts. Ask Guest Services whether your hotel can use them.
