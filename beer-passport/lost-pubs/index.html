The Lost Pubs of North Yorkshire

Every pub has a story. Some have simply stopped serving.

An archive of the county's inns, taverns, coaching houses and hotels, the ones still pouring and the ones that have gone quiet. It is built town by town from local histories, old directories and the memories of people who drank in them. The first collection is Boroughbridge, once a busy stop on the Great North Road.

Where the facts come from

Every entry names its sources in plain words: local histories, trade directories, Historic England listings and CAMRA's pub records. Where sources disagree, both are shown.

Only what's known

Pins go on the map only where a site's position is documented. Dates appear only where a record gives them. Gaps are listed as open questions, not filled in.

Help fill the gaps

Old photographs, landlords' names, menus and corrections are all welcome. Nothing is published until it has been checked and you've given permission.
